# Senior Backend Engineer - Screening Rubric

## Position Overview
Senior Backend Engineer role focused on building scalable, high-performance backend systems using modern technologies.

## Must-Have Requirements (Pass/Fail)
These are non-negotiable requirements. Candidates must meet ALL of these to proceed.

- [ ] 5+ years of professional backend development experience
- [ ] Strong proficiency in at least one backend language (Python, Java, Go, Node.js, etc.)
- [ ] Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, etc.)
- [ ] Experience with RESTful API design and implementation
- [ ] Understanding of software design patterns and principles
- [ ] Experience with version control (Git)
- [ ] Strong problem-solving and debugging skills

## Scoring Criteria (0-100 scale)

### Technical Skills (40 points)
- **Backend Languages & Frameworks** (15 points)
  - Expert level (13-15): Multiple languages, deep framework knowledge
  - Advanced level (10-12): Strong in primary language, good framework knowledge
  - Intermediate level (7-9): Competent in one language and framework
  - Below expectations (0-6): Limited experience or outdated technologies

- **Database & Data Modeling** (10 points)
  - Expert (9-10): Complex schema design, optimization, multiple DB types
  - Advanced (7-8): Good schema design, query optimization
  - Intermediate (5-6): Basic CRUD operations, simple schemas
  - Below expectations (0-4): Limited database experience

- **System Design & Architecture** (15 points)
  - Expert (13-15): Designed large-scale distributed systems
  - Advanced (10-12): Designed medium-scale systems, understands trade-offs
  - Intermediate (7-9): Understands basic architecture patterns
  - Below expectations (0-6): Limited architecture experience

### Experience & Impact (30 points)
- **Years of Experience** (10 points)
  - 8+ years: 10 points
  - 6-7 years: 8 points
  - 5 years: 6 points
  - Less than 5 years: 0 points (fails must-have)

- **Project Complexity & Scale** (10 points)
  - Led large-scale projects (9-10): Millions of users, high availability
  - Significant projects (7-8): Thousands of users, production systems
  - Medium projects (5-6): Small production systems
  - Limited scope (0-4): Personal projects or small internal tools

- **Leadership & Mentoring** (10 points)
  - Strong leader (9-10): Led teams, mentored juniors, technical decisions
  - Some leadership (6-8): Mentored others, code reviews
  - Individual contributor (3-5): Primarily solo work
  - No leadership (0-2): No mentoring or leadership experience

### Modern Practices (20 points)
- **DevOps & CI/CD** (7 points)
  - Expert (6-7): Docker, Kubernetes, full CI/CD pipelines
  - Advanced (4-5): Docker, basic CI/CD
  - Basic (2-3): Familiar with concepts
  - None (0-1): No DevOps experience

- **Testing & Quality** (7 points)
  - Excellent (6-7): TDD, comprehensive test coverage, automated testing
  - Good (4-5): Unit tests, integration tests
  - Basic (2-3): Some testing experience
  - Poor (0-1): Minimal testing

- **Cloud & Infrastructure** (6 points)
  - Expert (5-6): AWS/GCP/Azure, infrastructure as code
  - Advanced (3-4): Used cloud services, basic infrastructure
  - Basic (1-2): Minimal cloud experience
  - None (0): No cloud experience

### Communication & Soft Skills (10 points)
- **Communication** (5 points)
  - Excellent (5): Clear technical writing, presentations, documentation
  - Good (3-4): Can explain technical concepts clearly
  - Adequate (1-2): Basic communication
  - Poor (0): Unclear communication

- **Problem Solving & Learning** (5 points)
  - Excellent (5): Proactive, learns quickly, solves complex problems
  - Good (3-4): Good problem solver, continuous learner
  - Adequate (1-2): Can solve routine problems
  - Poor (0): Struggles with problem-solving

## Scoring Thresholds

- **90-100**: Exceptional candidate - Fast track to interview
- **75-89**: Strong candidate - Proceed to interview
- **60-74**: Borderline candidate - Additional screening questions recommended
- **Below 60**: Does not meet requirements - Reject

## Screening Questions to Ask

If the candidate passes the initial CV screening (must-haves met and score >= 60), ask these questions:

1. **System Design**: "Describe a complex backend system you designed. What were the key architectural decisions and trade-offs you considered?"

2. **Problem Solving**: "Tell us about a challenging technical problem you solved. What was your approach and what was the outcome?"

3. **Scalability**: "How would you design a system to handle 10x traffic growth? What bottlenecks would you anticipate and how would you address them?"

4. **Code Quality**: "What practices do you follow to ensure code quality and maintainability in your projects?"

5. **Learning & Growth**: "What new technologies or practices have you learned in the past year? How did you apply them?"

## Red Flags (Automatic Rejection)
- Fabricated experience or credentials
- Toxic behavior or unprofessional communication
- Unable to explain technical concepts from their own CV
- No hands-on coding experience (only theoretical knowledge)
- Unwilling to learn or adapt to new technologies

## Notes for AI Screening Agent
- Focus on concrete examples and measurable achievements
- Look for evidence of impact (metrics, scale, outcomes)
- Prioritize recent experience (last 3-5 years)
- Consider career progression and growth trajectory
- Be objective and avoid bias based on company names or education
