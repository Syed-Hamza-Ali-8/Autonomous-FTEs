# DevOps Lead - Scoring Rubric

## Must-Have Requirements
- 5+ years of DevOps/SRE/Infrastructure experience
- Strong experience with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, GitLab CI)
- Proficiency with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Pulumi)

## Nice-to-Have
- Monitoring and observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog)
- Configuration management (Ansible, Chef, Puppet)
- Security best practices and compliance
- Database administration and optimization
- Scripting skills (Python, Bash, Go)

## Scoring Breakout
- Skills & Technical Knowledge: 35 points
- Experience & Track Record: 25 points
- Project Impact & Scale: 20 points
- Communication & Leadership: 10 points
- Bonuses (certifications, open source): 10 points

## Red Flags
- No cloud platform experience
- No CI/CD or automation experience
- Short job-hopping history
- No evidence of production systems management
